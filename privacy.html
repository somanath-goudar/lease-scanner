Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Your Lease Text Is Never Uploaded

The Lease Red Flag Scanner performs all analysis entirely within your browser using JavaScript. Your lease text is never sent to any server, stored anywhere, or seen by anyone. You can disconnect from the internet after loading the page and the tool will still work.

No Personal Data Collected

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The tool has no user accounts, no login, and no contact form.

No Cookies or Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics scripts. The only browser storage used is localStorage to remember your dark/light mode preference — this stays on your device and is never transmitted.

No Third-Party Trackers

We do not embed any third-party tracking scripts (Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, etc.).

Children's Privacy

This tool is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
